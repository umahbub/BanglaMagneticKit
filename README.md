# 🧲 ছড়ার চুম্বক | Bangla Magnetic Word Kit for Kids

An interactive web platform inspired by magnetic poetry kits, designed specifically for Bangla (বাংলা) rhymes and poems (*Chora* / ছড়া). Built from the curated vocabulary, roots, endings, and 20 classic Bangla poems in [`bangla_choRar_shobdo.xlsx`](./bangla_choRar_shobdo.xlsx).

🌐 **Live Website (GitHub Pages):** [https://umahbub.github.io/BanglaMagneticKit/](https://umahbub.github.io/BanglaMagneticKit/)

---

## ✨ Key Features

1. **🎨 Browse 1,340+ Bangla Words & 37 Suffix/Ending Magnets (`শব্দের ঝুলি`)**
   - Organized into kid-friendly categories: 🌟 Popular (`জনপ্রিয়`), 🧩 Word Endings (`বিভক্তি ও প্রত্যয়`), 🙋 People & Pronouns (`মানুষ ও সর্বনাম`), 🌙 Nature & Sky (`প্রকৃতি ও আকাশ`), 🦜 Birds & Animals (`পাখি ও প্রাণী`), 🏃 Verbs (`কাজ ও ক্রিয়া`), 🎨 Adjectives & Colors (`রং ও বিশেষণ`), 🥁 Rhythm & Onomatopoeia (`ছন্দ ও ধ্বনি`), and 🏠 Objects & Food (`চারপাশ ও খাবার`).
   - Search in **Bangla Unicode** (`চাঁদ`, `পাখি`) or **English Phonetics** (`chaand`, `pakhi`), or filter by Bangla alphabet letters (`অ আ ই ঈ ক খ গ ঘ...`).

2. **🧩 Smart Bangla Suffix & Ending Fusion (`বিভক্তি ও প্রত্যয় সংযোজন`)**
   - Drag any suffix magnet (`-এ`, `-ই`, `-আ`, `-ও`, `-য়`, `-তে`, `-কে`, `-র`, `-এর`, `-দের`, `-টা`, `-টি`, `-গুলো`, `-গুলি`, `-ছি`, `-ছো`, `-ছে`, `-লাম`, `-লে`, `-লো`, `-বো`, `-বে`, `-ইয়ে`, etc.) onto a word tile on the board to automatically fuse it with proper Bangla vowel-sign (*Kar* / কার) rules:
     - `মাছ` + `-এ` ➔ `মাছে`
     - `বুন` + `-এছি` ➔ `বুনেছি`
     - `বেঁধে` + `-ছে` ➔ `বেঁধেছে`
     - `চাঁদ` + `-র` ➔ `চাঁদের`
   - Detach/peel off endings anytime with a single click (`✂️ মূল শব্দে ফিরুন`).

3. **🏃 Interactive Subject-Verb Agreement (`কর্তা অনুযায়ী ক্রিয়ার রূপ পরিবর্তন`)**
   - Click any word tile on the board to open the **Word Inspector Dock**:
     - Switch between subjects: **আমি/আমরা** (1st Person), **তুমি/তোমরা** (2nd Familiar), **তুই/তোরা** (2nd Intimate), **সে/তারা** (3rd Person), and **আপনি/তিনি** (Honorific).
     - Pick across 6 tenses: Present Simple, Present Continuous, Present Perfect, Simple Past, Past Continuous, Future, and Participles.
   - **🤝 Auto Subject-Verb Agreement (`স্বয়ংক্রিয় কর্তা-ক্রিয়া মিল`)**: Automatically harmonizes verbs on a line to match the subject pronoun (`আমি`, `তুমি`, `তুই`, `সে`, `আপনি`) on that same line!

4. **🔊 Bangla Audio Pronunciation (Single Word & Full Poem Narration)**
   - Click `🔊` on any word tile in the browser or inspector to hear its Bangla pronunciation.
   - Click **`▶️ ছড়া শোনো (Play Poem)`** to hear your entire poem read aloud line by line with synchronized visual tile bouncing and live bilingual subtitles!

5. **Abc English Phonetic Spelling (`ইংরেজি উচ্চারণ বানান`)**
   - Toggleable English phonetic transliteration underneath every Bangla magnet tile (`/aay aay chaand mama/`), helping learners and bilingual kids read with confidence.

6. **🎵 Rhyme Finder (`ছন্দ মেলাও`) & 📖 20 Classic Bangla Choras (`ছড়ার ঝুলি`)**
   - Find rhyming Bangla words for any word on your board with one click.
   - Load and remix 20 classic Bangla poems from `bangla_choRar_shobdo.xlsx` (*আয় আয় চাঁদ মামা*, *আয়রে আয় টিয়ে*, *নোটন নোটন পায়রাগুলি*, *বৃষ্টি পড়ে টাপুর টুপুর*, *বাবুরাম সাপুড়ে*, *ট্রেন*, *তালগাছ*, *আমি হব*, *লিচুচোর*, and more!).

7. **➕ Custom Word Creator & 📸 Downloadable Poem Card (PNG)**
   - Add custom Bangla words with auto-generated phonetics and inflections saved to your browser.
   - Download your finished poem as a colorful high-resolution PNG card!
